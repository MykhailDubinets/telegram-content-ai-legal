AI content workflow for Telegram channels

Turn Telegram news into social content.

Tik News Open Bot helps channel administrators transform news into structured visual content, review it, and publish approved content to connected social-media accounts.

What the service does

The service is designed around a Telegram-first content workflow. AI assists with preparation while the administrator remains in control of the content and publishing step.

AI-assisted content

Process incoming Telegram news and create structured social-media copy and content formats.

Visual news cards

Generate branded news cards and carousel-ready visual content from the prepared story.

Video content

Combine visual cards into vertical slideshow videos with transitions and motion for social platforms.

Review before publishing

Content can be sent back to Telegram for review and approval before a publishing action.

Social integrations

Connect supported social-media accounts through their official APIs and publish approved content.

Channel workflow

Keep news collection, content generation, review, and publishing in one Telegram-centered workflow.

How it works

A typical content flow from a Telegram channel to an approved social-media post.

01News arrives

A channel post is received by the bot.

02AI prepares it

Text and available media are processed into a content structure.

03Visuals are created

Cards, carousels, or video assets can be generated.

04User reviews

The administrator reviews and approves the content.

05Publish

Approved content is sent through a connected social API.

TikTok integration

Tik News Open Bot is being integrated with TikTok's official Content Posting API so administrators can connect their own TikTok account and publish approved video content.

User control
The service is designed so that the account owner controls the connected TikTok account and approves content before a publishing action. TikTok account access is handled through TikTok's authorization flow.

About Tik News Open Bot

Tik News Open Bot is an independent content workflow tool for Telegram channel administrators. It combines automation, AI-assisted preparation, visual generation, and social publishing integrations in a single workflow.
