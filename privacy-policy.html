Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tik News Open Bot ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use Tik News Open Bot and related services ("Services").

Tik News Open Bot is a Telegram-based content creation service for administrators of Telegram channels. The Service can process Telegram content and use third-party services to generate news cards and publish content to social media platforms, including TikTok and Instagram.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process:

  • Telegram user ID, username, first name, and other Telegram profile information made available to the bot;
  • Telegram channel information and channel identifiers for channels connected to the Service;
  • News posts, text, images, and other content that you submit to or make available to the Service;
  • Information necessary to connect and operate supported social media accounts;
  • Messages and information you send to us when contacting support.

Social media account information

If you connect an Instagram or TikTok account, we may process information provided by the relevant platform through its APIs, including account identifiers, profile information, access tokens, permissions, and information necessary to publish content on your behalf.

Technical information

We may process technical information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service, such as error logs, request information, and basic usage information.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • operate and provide the Service;
  • process Telegram channel posts and generate content;
  • create images, carousels, and video content;
  • publish content to connected TikTok and Instagram accounts when requested or authorized by you;
  • authenticate connected accounts and maintain authorized connections;
  • provide support and communicate with users;
  • monitor usage, diagnose errors, maintain security, and prevent abuse;
  • improve the Service and understand how its features are used;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Artificial Intelligence

The Service uses third-party artificial intelligence technologies, including services provided by OpenAI, to process content and generate or transform content.

Depending on the feature you use, text, images, or other content submitted to the Service may be processed by an AI service provider in order to provide the requested functionality.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information for AI processing. Users should not submit sensitive personal information to the Service unless it is necessary and appropriate for the requested functionality.

4. Social Media APIs

Instagram

If you connect an Instagram account, the Service may use Meta/Instagram APIs to authenticate the account and publish content that you have authorized the Service to publish.

TikTok

If you connect a TikTok account, the Service may use TikTok APIs to authenticate the account and publish content that you have authorized the Service to publish.

We do not sell social media account information or use connected social media accounts for purposes unrelated to providing the Service.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information with third-party service providers when this is necessary to provide the Service. These providers may include:

  • AI service providers, including OpenAI;
  • Telegram and infrastructure used to operate Telegram bots;
  • Meta/Instagram services when you connect and use Instagram publishing;
  • TikTok services when you connect and use TikTok publishing;
  • hosting, storage, analytics, security, and other technical service providers used to operate the Service.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the security of the Service, to prevent fraud or abuse, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of business assets.

6. Social Media Access Tokens

When you authorize the Service to connect to a supported social media account, the Service may receive access credentials or tokens from the relevant platform.

These credentials are used to provide the authorized functionality, such as publishing content to the connected account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect such credentials from unauthorized access.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

Generated content and temporary technical files may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the requested functionality and maintain the Service.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal information.

To exercise your rights, contact us using the email address below. We will consider requests in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for users who are legally permitted to use the Service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we may update the date shown at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.
